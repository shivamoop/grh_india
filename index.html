CDSCO & DGHS Policy Notification

A Landmark Step Toward Affordable & Inclusive Healthcare

The Government of India and the Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO) have unified the Goods and Services Tax (GST) on medical devices, dermal matrices, and pharmaceuticals, reducing it from 12% to 5%.

As an enterprise committed to the promise of human potential, GRH is passing these tax savings directly to our partner hospitals, clinics, and surgical patients. This ensures that advanced wound therapies and dermal regeneration matrices are more accessible across all income brackets and regional hospitals.
